i got 1.2M views in 18 days as an animated character with claude (full guide)

4 min read

0 to 1.2M views in 18 days.

i didn't use a camera or hire anyone.. i just took what's working and improved it LMAO (more on that below).

if you're in a rush, follow @vidguyai + @bizdevelo and bookmark this for later.

if you'd like to chat with me directly, my DMs are always open.

what's in this article:

  • how i clone myself (or anyone who said yes) into a cartoon
  • the one prompt that writes, voices and animates a short
  • where to drop real-looking ai clips so people stop scrolling
  • how to run it on autopilot with 25+ tiktok & instagram accounts
  • the exact prompts i use, ready to paste

full disclosure: i'm the co-founder of @vidguyai, which is exactly why you're getting my numbers and the whole setup instead of me just telling you it works.

the idea

plenty of accounts are blowing up right now using this style which i've been posting in silence for the past 2 months.

take this one for example -

i made accounts just like this one, not just for myself but for famous youtubers, too. they loved the idea and gave me the green lights to pump out as much content of them using this format as i felt like doing.

and who would've thought.. they all pulled in millions of views.

why cartoon style?

valid question.

slideshows get reach on tiktok but not on instagram. i need formats that work cross-platform + people follow a recurring character, the same way kids remember a cereal mascot.

a cartoon also gets a pass that a talking head doesn't. it doesn't read as an ad, and since it's obviously ai, the is-this-real comments don't come.

i built the setup in claude, and the whole workflow now lives inside the vidguy mcp, so you don't have to build any of it yourself.

step 1: connect the vidguy mcp (2 minutes, once)

there's nothing to install. you need claude and a vidguy account.

  1. in claude, go to settings → connectors, add the vidguy mcp and sign in (you can find it on vidguy(dot)ai/mcp)
  2. ask it to "check my vidguy credits". if you get a number back you're connected
  3. tell it "use vidguy mcp to create a brand from [your website]". it reads your site once, so every short already knows what you sell and who buys it

the animated shorts skill lives inside the mcp, which means claude pulls the templates and scene styles straight from vidguy every time. so if we update the templates, your next short uses the new ones.

step 2: clone yourself

start by cloning yourself or anyone on your team who you'd like to appear on the videos.

pro-tip from me: make your character have features that your target audience might know from other famous creators in your n

use vidguy mcp to clone me into an animated character.
here's my photo [attach] and a 30 second voice recording [attach].
i'm the expert in my cast.

vidguy saves the cast to your account. every short after that uses the same faces and voices.

step 3: one prompt

use vidguy mcp to create an animated short for [your product / topic].

vidguy pulls your brand, writes a 30-60 sec script and claude shows it to you first. change whatever you want.

after you say 'go' it splits the script into scenes and voices every line with the right character. then it animates the whole thing and makes one or two real-looking ai clips for the proof moments (it tells you the credit cost before spending anything).

you get a preview back. i usually have notes like "slow down scene 3" or "make the skeptic angrier" and it just fixes them. i also like giving more specific instructions but those are for another article. final export is a vertical short with a caption ready to go.

each short only takes about 6 minutes from prompt to final output.

step 4: the skeleton

every short follows the same skeleton, and you can see it in pretty much any of these reel. knowing it makes your notes better.

first 1.5 seconds: the hook. the founder hits the problem in big text and promises a payoff, something like "i'll give you the checklist at the end".

then the problem, one beat at a time until about 20 seconds in, with a counter in the corner so people stick around to see where it lands. the skeptic asks whatever the viewer is thinking.

around 20 seconds it goes dark and the expert says one word, like "the word is attempts".

last 5 seconds are the fix, that's where the cartoon cuts to the ai clip. end card asks for a comment keyword and a follow so the dm lands.

pacing is roughly 3 words a second, with something moving on screen every second or so.

step 5: the ai clips

one or two per short, 2 to 4 seconds each. more than that and it starts feeling like an ad again.

vidguy picks where they go by default. if you want a specific shot:

use vidguy mcp to add a 4 second clip to scene 4: [what we see, camera, light].
tell me the credit cost and wait for my go.

it picks the video model and matches the clip's first frame to the cartoon's colors. the character keeps talking over the clip.

step 6: autopilot

i cut every short into a few versions (new hook, or a different ai clip) and spread them across accounts. the algo buries duplicate files, so every version has to differ.

and if you don't have accounts yet, the vidguy mcp comes with hosted tiktok and instagram accounts that vidguy runs for you:

use vidguy mcp to list my hosted accounts and how much posting capacity they have left.
then schedule this week's animated shorts across them, max 2 a day per account.
show me the schedule before anything goes live.

you approve the schedule once and it posts on its own. each hosted account posts up to twice a day and gets warmed up before its first post.

the prompts i actually use

start with the trap list. the counter is what keeps people watching to the end:

use vidguy mcp to create a trap list animated short for [your product], 35 seconds.
hook (the founder): "[outcome next to zero]. i'll give you the checklist at the end."
counter: "[thing that counts]".
five traps: [trap 1] ... [trap 5].
the skeptic asks: "[the viewer's objection]".
reframe: "the word is [one word]."
ai clip of [the fix, for real]. end card: comment [KEYWORD].

the skeptic one works for anything people don't believe:

use vidguy mcp to create a skeptic animated short for [your product], 30 seconds.
the skeptic: "[the claim everyone doubts] is fake."
the founder tries it, ai clip of [the result]. the expert explains the one reason it works.
end card: comment [KEYWORD].

and once you know what works, batch it:

use vidguy mcp to create 5 trap list animated shorts for [your product] from these topics: [topics].
show me all 5 scripts before anything gets voiced.

closing thoughts

the four tools and the lost weekend are gone. what's left is deciding who your cast is and what they argue about, and that's the part i still spend the most time on.
