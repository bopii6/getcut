# Credits & Billing

Credits are based on duration and the content sources you choose.

## How credits are calculated

- Longer videos use more credits
- Premium content sources use more credits
- You see the estimate before generating

## How to use credits efficiently

1. Start with **Balanced** presets.
2. Keep early drafts to 15–30 seconds.
3. Upgrade to Premium only when you need unique visuals.

## Ads on demand

[Ads on demand](https://www.vidguy.ai/docs/ads-on-demand) is priced per ad: 1 credit to read the page, then 6 per static ad (2 variants), 25 per UGC or green-screen ad, 37 per animated ad and 50 per music-video ad, plus 1 to render the files.

## Token snacks

A [token snack pack](https://www.vidguy.ai/docs/token-snacks) is 25 credits per call, or 18 without the `ai-ugc` video, plus 3 to render the files.


  If you need higher throughput, the Pro and Enterprise plans include larger monthly credit pools.
