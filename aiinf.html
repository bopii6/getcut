AI influencer FAQ

What is an AI influencer?

+

An AI influencer is a made up person or character that posts content like a real creator. On VidGuy it keeps the same face, name and personality in every photo, carousel and video, so your audience can get to know it.

How do I create an AI influencer?

+

Sign up, open the Companion and create an influencer. Pick realistic, animated 3D or anime, choose the look or upload up to 4 photos, and generate a preview for 1 credit. Then give it a name, a niche and a personality, and build the starter vault for 40 credits.

Will the face stay the same in every post?

+

Yes. VidGuy builds an identity sheet first and makes new content from it and the vault, so the face, hair and style match from post to post.

Can I use my own photos?

+

Yes. Upload up to 4 photos of the same person and they set the face. Only use photos of yourself or of someone who agreed to it.

Can it be a cartoon or a mascot instead of a person?

+

Yes. Pick animated 3D or anime in the builder. Realistic influencers are placed into real footage, and animated ones are animated, so both get videos.

Does it post on its own?

+

No. Every piece lands on your Review board first. Approve it to post now, pick a day, ask for changes or reject it. You can set schedules so it makes drafts on its own (3 on Starter, 10 on Pro), but posting always needs your OK.

What content can it make?

+

Carousels, text posts, memes, UGC videos, b-roll demos and images. A mixed content pack costs 25 credits. Five pieces of one type cost 18. You can also just ask for something in chat.

How much does it cost?

+

Plans start at $49 a month with 300 credits. A preview costs 1 credit, the starter vault 40 credits, and content packs 18 or 25 credits.

How many AI influencers can I have?

+

Starter keeps 2 creator agents with 1 slot saved for an influencer. Pro keeps 6 and Scale keeps 11. Influencers share these slots with clippers and faceless creators.

Can I create an AI influencer from Claude or ChatGPT?

+

Yes. Connect the VidGuy MCP server and ask. Claude or ChatGPT can create the influencer, build its vault, make content packs and chat with it for you.
